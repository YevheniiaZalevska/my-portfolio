Bistro Capibara

  • Landing
  • Animations
  • Responsive

A website for a bistro located at the Parrot House in Gdańsk, designed to showcase the venue and its menu. The website features 8 menu sections with food photography, including pizza, pasta, Asian dishes, soups, salads, and more. Special attention is given to interactive visual effects: pizza ingredients assemble on screen, a tomato rolls towards a fork, and food photography responds to cursor movements and scrolling. Visitors can also find information about the venue and easily place orders by phone or through Pyszne and Glovo.

Sealine

  • Landing
  • Calculator
  • Animations

A landing page for Sealine, a company specializing in short-term and long-term rental management in Gdańsk. The website helps property owners explore the benefits of the service, estimate potential rental income using an interactive calculator, and submit an inquiry. The design combines atmospheric interior photography, elegant typography, and scroll-triggered animations to create a premium hospitality-inspired experience. The website is fully responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
